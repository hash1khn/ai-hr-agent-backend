# Acme Technologies — Leave Policy

This policy describes leave entitlements for employees of Acme Technologies. Individual remaining balances are maintained by HR and are not listed in this document.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **24 annual leaves per year**.

Leave is accrued monthly. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours.

## Other leave types

- Sick leave: 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required after 2 consecutive sick days.
- Casual leave: 5 days per year.
- Maternity leave: 16 weeks.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days.

## Applying for leave

Submit leave requests to your manager by email at least 3 working days in advance, except sick leave. Copy hr@acme.example.

Public holidays published by the company each year are in addition to annual leave.

## Probation

During the first 3 months of employment, annual leave is unpaid. Sick leave may still be used.

This document does not describe unused-leave payout, sabbatical leave, or unpaid leave beyond probation.
