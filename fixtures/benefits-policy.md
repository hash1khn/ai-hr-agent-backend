# Acme Technologies — Benefits Policy

Benefits below apply to confirmed employees after successful completion of probation, unless a section says otherwise.

## Health

Confirmed employees are enrolled in the company outpatient health plan. A spouse and up to 2 children may be added within 30 days of confirmation.

## Life insurance

Group life cover equals 24 months of basic salary.

## Learning

Employees may claim up to PKR 30,000 per year for job-related training. Claims require manager approval and receipts.

## Communications allowance

Field staff receive a monthly communications allowance of PKR 2,500. Office-based staff do not receive this allowance.

## Notice period

The notice period is 30 days for confirmed staff and 7 days during probation.
