# Acme Technologies — Attendance Policy

## Expected attendance

Employees are expected to be present during office hours unless they are on approved leave or an approved remote day.

- Office hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday
- Arrival after 9:30 AM without prior notice is recorded as late
- Three late arrivals in a calendar month may result in a written reminder from the manager

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote days must be requested at least 1 working day in advance.

## Absence

Unplanned absence must be reported to the manager by 9:30 AM on the same day, with hr@acme.example copied.

Absence of 2 consecutive days without notice may be treated as unauthorized leave.

## Time tracking

Employees record attendance in the office check-in sheet or the approved digital attendance form. Managers review attendance weekly.
