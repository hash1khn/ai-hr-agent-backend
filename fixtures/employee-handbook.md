# Acme Technologies — Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Technologies. It summarizes workplace expectations. If a topic is not covered here, contact HR.

## Working hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday.

- Office hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time
- Lunch break: 1 hour, typically 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM
- Full-time employees work 40 hours per week
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM

Flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM are allowed with a manager's written approval. Employees who start later must still complete 8 working hours.

## Code of conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited.

Confidential company information, including customer data and unpublished product plans, must not be shared outside Acme Technologies.

## How to apply for leave

Leave requests are submitted to your manager by email at least 3 working days in advance, except for sick leave.

Copy hr@acme.example on every leave request.

## Probation

New employees complete a 3-month probation period. During probation, annual leave is unpaid. Sick leave remains available.
